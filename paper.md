A Simulation-Based Consistency Study of the Dark Vital Dimensional Hypothesis

Abstract

We present a simulation-focused numerical framework designed to test the internal consistency and parameter sensitivity of the Dark Vital Dimensional Hypothesis (DVDH). The framework implements a microphysical instability proxy, MCMC-based inference, and null-model controls without asserting empirical validation.
