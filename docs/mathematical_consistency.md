Mathematical Consistency of DVDH

Dimensional Normalization

All governing equations are expressed in dimensionless Π-groups to ensure scale invariance and numerical stability across simulation regimes.

Classical Limit

The DVDH framework is constructed to reduce exactly to classical General Relativity in the limit:

alpha_VX → 0
F_VX → 0

Under this limit, all additional coupling terms vanish identically.

Stability Constraints

- Energy density remains positive definite
- No runaway solutions observed within bounded parameter space
- Time evolution satisfies numerical Lyapunov stability criteria

Internal Coherence
