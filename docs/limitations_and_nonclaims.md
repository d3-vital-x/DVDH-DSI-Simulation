Limitations & Non-Claims

This document explicitly defines what the DVDH–DSI–Simulation does not claim.

---

Non-Empirical Status

- This repository does not present experimental confirmation
- No observational detection is asserted
- No astrophysical object is claimed to be explained conclusively

---

Simulation-Bound Constructs

- DSI is treated as a numerical instability proxy
- Thresholds are model-defined, not observed constants
- Parameter mappings are theoretical consistency tools

---

No Cosmological Assertions

- No claims about early-universe physics
- No dark energy replacement proposal
- No modification of General Relativity is asserted

---

Intended Use

This codebase is intended for:

- Hypothesis stress-testing
- Internal consistency validation
- Parameter sensitivity exploration

It is not intended as evidence of physical discovery.

---

Reviewer Note

Any interpretation beyond the above scope constitutes misuse of this framework and is not endorsed by the authors.
